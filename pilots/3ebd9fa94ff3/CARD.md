## Proposal — current state

Minerva verification layer currently uses SpanUQ-lite self-consistency voting and cross-model agreement (cross_agreement.py) to reduce hallucinations, but does not explicitly anchor reasoning traces to constraints.

## Proposal — proposed change

Add a constraint-anchoring step to the Minerva verification pipeline: after generating a verification trace, parse and extract explicit constraints from the query, then check that the trace's intermediate steps satisfy these constraints before final acceptance. This could be implemented as a new module (e.g., minerva_constraint_anchor.py) that hooks into the existing verification flow, possibly as a pre-filter or post-check.
