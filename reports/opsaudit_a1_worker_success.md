Within-dispatch retry (attempt 1 -> attempt 2, the actual MAX_ATTEMPTS=2 mechanism): 76 pairs.
- 76/76 (100%) reused the IDENTICAL max_turns ceiling on attempt 2 — there is no auto-escalation today.
- 53/76 (70%) repeated the EXACT SAME failure classification on attempt 2.
- 3/76 (4%) succeeded on attempt 2 with the SAME ceiling as the failed attempt 1.

Across ALL attempts on a ticket (including cross-cycle re-dispatch after config changes, attempts 3/4+): 69/102 (68%) tickets ever succeeded. 25 on attempt 1, 44 needed a later attempt — 36/44 (82%) of those later successes ran under a DIFFERENT max_turns than the attempt immediately before them, so a ceiling change, not the repeat itself, is what the recoveries have in common.

**Conclusion**: MAX_ATTEMPTS=2 under an unchanged ceiling recovered 3/76 (4%) of retries — the real recovery lever measured in this data is raising max_turns before the retry, not retrying per se.
