## 4. Does attempt 2 ever succeed after attempt 1 fails?

Attempt N -> attempt N+1 classification transitions, all N (fails only):
| prev | next | count |
|---|---|---|
| KILLED | KILLED | 23 |
| TURNS | TURNS | 14 |
| KILLED | TURNS | 11 |
| TURNS | KILLED | 10 |
| FALSE_COMPLETE | FALSE_COMPLETE | 7 |
| KILLED | SUCCESS | 5 |
| KILLED | FALSE_COMPLETE | 4 |
| FALSE_COMPLETE | SUCCESS | 2 |
| TURNS | SUCCESS | 1 |
| FALSE_COMPLETE | KILLED | 1 |

Within-dispatch retry (attempt 1 -> attempt 2, the actual MAX_ATTEMPTS=2 mechanism): 62 pairs.
- 62/62 reused the IDENTICAL max_turns ceiling on attempt 2 — there is no auto-escalation today.
- 39/62 repeated the EXACT SAME failure classification on attempt 2 (KILLED->KILLED or TURNS->TURNS dominate).
- 3/62 (4.8%) succeeded on attempt 2 with the SAME ceiling as the failed attempt 1.

Across ALL attempts on a ticket (including cross-cycle re-dispatch after config changes, attempts 3/4+): 14/69 tickets ever succeeded. 6 on attempt 1, 8 needed a later attempt — but every one of those later successes came from a re-dispatch under a DIFFERENT (usually higher) max_turns, hours after the original 2-attempt cycle, not from the automatic same-ceiling retry.

**Conclusion**: MAX_ATTEMPTS=2 under an unchanged ceiling is close to pure waste (3/62 same-ceiling retries recovered) — the real recovery lever measured in this data is raising max_turns before the retry, not retrying per se.

## 6. Recommended ARCHETYPE_MAX_TURNS and MAX_ATTEMPTS

Current state (2026-08-22): ceilings were already lifted to 600 for every archetype as a stopgap (`fix(harness): lift turn ceilings until real fix`) pending the mechanics audit that will count real assistant turns instead of hook frames. The numbers below are what the ceiling SHOULD converge to once that accounting fix lands — sized off measured SUCCESS turns_used with headroom for the turn-count drift measured in §2, not off the current 600 placeholder.

| archetype | n success | max observed success turns | recommended ceiling | confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mechanical-edit | 3 | 27 | 40 | low (n<5) |
| novel-design | 2 | 81 | 110 | low (n<5) |
| probe-build | 1 | 22 | 30 | low (n<5) |
| rca-investigation | 1 | 100 | 130 | low (n<5) |
| research-synthesis | 1 | 118 | 150 | low (n<5) |
| standard-engineering | 8 | 141 | 180 | medium |
| research | 0 | n/a | keep >=120, re-measure after first success | NONE — 1 attempt, zero successes |
| verify-pass | 0 | n/a | keep >=120, re-measure after first success | NONE — 12 attempts, zero successes |

`verify-pass` and `research` (n=1, mislabeled `research-synthesis`?) archetypes have zero measured successes in this dataset — that is a bigger problem than any ceiling number, and should block on its own investigation rather than a turns bump.

**MAX_ATTEMPTS**: keep at 2, but make attempt 2's max_turns escalate over attempt 1's when attempt 1 failed via TURNS or KILLED/turns_exceeded (e.g. x1.5) instead of re-dispatching under the identical ceiling that just failed. §4 measured that today attempt 2 changes nothing (62/62 same ceiling) and only recovers ~5% of retries; every recorded recovery beyond attempt 2 in the full dataset came from a ceiling change, not a repeat.

### Falsifiable predictions

1. If ARCHETYPE_MAX_TURNS is set per the table above (once real assistant-turn counting lands), FALSE_COMPLETE rate for standard-engineering will drop below its current 16/79 (20%) attempt-level rate, because fewer runs will be forcibly killed mid-task and then misreport partial state as done on the next attempt (§3's KILLED-then-FALSE_COMPLETE pattern). Falsified if FALSE_COMPLETE rate is unchanged or higher after 20 more standard-engineering attempts at the new ceilings.
2. If attempt 2 escalates max_turns by >=1.5x on a TURNS/KILLED attempt 1 instead of repeating it, the attempt-2 success rate will exceed the measured 5% (3/62) same-ceiling baseline. Falsified if the next 20 escalated retries succeed at <=5%.
3. verify-pass's 0/12 success rate is NOT a turns problem (12 attempts spanned max_turns 25-120 with no successes at any ceiling) — raising its ceiling alone will not move its success rate. Falsified if a verify-pass ticket succeeds at a ceiling <=120 with no other change.
